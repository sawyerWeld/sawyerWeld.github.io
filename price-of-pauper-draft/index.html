Two honest numbers

The index and the shopping bill measure different things.

The raw basket cost can jump when membership changes. The chain-linked index removes that mechanical jump, then tracks the new basket until the next annual reconstitution.

02

The 2026 basket

Membership

The 2026 basket uses 18,772 MTGTop8 Pauper decklists from 2025. A nonbasic qualifies at 20 decks and 2.00% prevalence. Main deck and sideboard appearances both count; each card receives one unit. Membership updates each January from the prior year.

Historical series

Each month uses the cheapest exact-name normal nonfoil printing in MTGGoldfish’s paper history, which predominantly reflects TCGplayer Mid. Collector and World Championship editions are excluded.

Current Low

Today’s check uses the cheapest active English normal nonfoil TCGplayer listing at any condition, excluding shipping. It is a price floor, not a one-cart purchase total.

Score is prevalence divided by the 2% floor. It does not change a card’s one-copy weight.

07

Index rules

Simple enough to reproduce, strict enough to update.

Eligibility

A nonbasic card must appear in at least 1% and at least 20 of the previous calendar year’s recorded Pauper decks. Main decks and sideboards both count.

Price

One normal nonfoil copy at the cheapest recorded tournament-playable printing’s TCGplayer Mid price. Prices are sampled monthly.

Chain

Monthly returns use the same members on both sides. January additions and removals are divisor-neutral, so changing membership cannot create inflation by itself.

Historical source: MTGGoldfish paper price history. Current Low: live TCGplayer listings. Replacement-cost benchmarks, not player spending.
