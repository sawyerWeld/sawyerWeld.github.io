Eleven years of Paupergeddon

The Price of Pauper

Pauper limits rarity, not scarcity. Across 29 editions of Europe’s largest Pauper tournament, the price of the format’s enduring staples has nearly quadrupled, and the bill is concentrated in a short list of old commons.

Data and analysis by Sawyer Welden · Event-date prices in USD · Updated September 2026

02

What a deck costs now

The two newest editions are the first with exact field-wide card evidence.

Spring provides exact aggregate quantities for 965 lists. Summer preserves every individual list, so its median and middle 50% can be calculated rather than estimated.

Replacement cost, not player outlay. Existing collections, loans, local currencies, shipping, and chosen printings can materially change what any person paid.

05

The Universes Beyond premium

External-IP commons were a small share of cards but a costlier share of the basket.

Using Spring 2026 quantities and event-date prices, Universes Beyond cards cost more per submitted copy than cards set inside Magic’s own universe.

07

Methods and sources

A reproducible estimate, with its limits left visible.

Prices

Daily MTGGoldfish TCGplayer Mid history. For each event date, the collector chooses the cheapest recorded normal, nonfoil, tournament-playable paper printing. Ordinary basics are $0.

Deck evidence

Spring 2026 uses exact aggregate card distributions; Summer 2026 uses all 1,069 individual lists. The event ledger links the surviving Top 8, Top 16, metagame, and full-field sources.

Interpretation

The fixed basket is an inflation index, not a historical field average. Deck dollar values are replacement-cost estimates and cover 97% or more of submitted copies.
