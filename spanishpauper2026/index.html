The first Spanish Pauper Championship brought 247 players to Ortuella, Bizkaia, making it the largest Pauper event played with Hawkeye's Bow + Seeker of Skybreak still legal. That makes the tournament a useful snapshot of the format right before the ban: Naya Bowseeker reached the finals, and combo decks in general performed well, with Ruby Storm and X Lands Spy converting strongly into Day 2.
