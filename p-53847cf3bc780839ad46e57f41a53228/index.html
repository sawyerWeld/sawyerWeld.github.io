What counts as a trophy?

One trophy for each completed FNM with at least three wins, zero losses, and zero draws. Official standings determine the record, including bye wins. Multiple players can trophy on the same night, regardless of tiebreakers. A 2–0 finish or any finish with a draw does not count. The Win-a-Box is outside this FNM trophy leaderboard.
