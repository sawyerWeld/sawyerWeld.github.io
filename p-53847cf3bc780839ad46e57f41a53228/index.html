How Elo works

Players start at 1200. Match results update ratings with K = 32; a draw counts as half a win. All matches in a round use the ratings at the start of that round. Byes do not affect Elo. Lines begin at a player’s first event; dashed sections carry their rating through events they missed.

The August 29 Win-a-Box includes Swiss and recorded quarterfinals. Later playoff rounds are not recorded. Event spacing is equal in the event view; the round view gives each recorded round equal space. Ratings are carried forward, with no inactivity penalty.

What counts as a trophy?

One trophy for each completed FNM with at least three wins, zero losses, and zero draws. Official standings determine the record, including bye wins. Multiple players can trophy on the same night, regardless of tiebreakers. A 2–0 finish or any finish with a draw does not count. The Win-a-Box is outside this FNM trophy leaderboard.
