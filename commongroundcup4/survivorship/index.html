Method

Seven Swiss rounds, then a Top 8

The chart follows the 100-player field through the seven Swiss rounds. A player remains alive while their record is X-1-1 or better: each loss adds three points of deficit from perfect and each draw adds two, with five points allowed. The Top 8 marker uses the organizer’s actual cut after Round 7.

Pairings, results, and decklists come from TopDeck. Archetypes are normalized from the published mainboards so closely related lists stay in a single readable band; distinctive brews retain their own names.
