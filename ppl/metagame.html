Counting

Popularity counts one entry per player per event, including unknown decks in the denominator. Win rate is (wins + half of draws) ÷ matches. Byes and confirmed intentional draws are excluded. Overall deck records include mirrors and matches against unknown decks. Mirror matchups are hidden in the matrix; games with an unknown deck cannot be assigned to a matchup. Each off-diagonal cell counts matches once from its row deck’s perspective. Small samples are descriptive, not a deck ranking.

Archetypes use reviewed mappings. Unreviewed labels remain separate. Expand a deck to see the original reported names.
